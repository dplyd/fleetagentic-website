The company behind Fleet Agentic.

Fleet Agentic builds fully managed on-premises AI for organizations whose data can't leave the building — law firms, health systems, defense contractors, and school districts.

Fleet Agentic was formerly known as DPLYD.

Fleet Agentic was founded by Kendall Miller to close the gap between frontier AI productivity and the data-handling obligations of regulated organizations. The founding observation: every "compliant" cloud AI product still asks you to ship your most sensitive content — or an index derived from it — to somebody else's computer. Fleet Agentic's answer is to move the computer instead.

The name describes the product: a fleet of AI agents, working as a team, in formation, on hardware you control. We install and operate the hardware; you own the boundary. Every demo is given by the founder, on your own data.
