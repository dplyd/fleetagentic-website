The hidden leak

Keeping the file local isn’t enough. The index is the leak.

Most “on-prem” AI keeps the document local, then ships a search index of it to the cloud. That creates a second, queryable copy of your sensitive data off-site.

The original file never moved. The sensitive information did.

CHUNKS
Verbatim passages lifted straight out of the document. Not a summary. The text itself.
EMBEDDINGS
Encode the meaning, and are reversible enough to reconstruct the source.
KEYWORDS
Client names, matter numbers, program names and codenames, in plaintext.
METADATA
Authors, dates, case IDs, file paths: a map of everything you hold.
RELATIONSHIPS
Which parties, entities and documents connect. Disclosure without a single sentence leaving.

Fleet Agentic runs on physical hardware inside your perimeter. The model, the index, the embeddings and the logs all stay on the same side of the boundary as your files.

Questions

What security teams ask first.

Does any data leave my network?

No. The model, the retrieval index, the embeddings and the logs all live on-premises, inside your perimeter. Nothing derived from your documents is sent to a third-party API.

How is this different from private cloud or GovCloud AI?

GovCloud and private-cloud AI still run on someone else’s computer. Your content and its search index are processed and stored outside your walls, under someone else’s controls. Fleet Agentic moves the computer to you instead.

Does it work in an air-gapped network?

Yes, on request. Fleet Agentic can be deployed fully disconnected. Managed updates arrive on a schedule and by a method that fits your environment, including offline transfer for air-gapped enclaves.

Who patches and audits it?

We manage patching, monitoring and updates of the software under the monthly fee, and of the hardware on the leased plan. Sentinel’s audit logs are available to your team and your assessor.

What does it cost?

On hardware you own: $500/month including 1 seat, then $25 per additional seat. Fully leased: $5,000/month flat with as many seats as the hardware supports, including hardware, maintenance, patching and support. No per-token metering on either.
