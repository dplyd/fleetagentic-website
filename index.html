FAQ

Straight answers.

Q ▸ Does any data leave my network?

No. The model, the retrieval index, the embeddings, and the logs all live on-premises, inside your perimeter. Nothing derived from your documents is transmitted to a third-party API.

Q ▸ What is a knowledge control plane?

The layer that decides who your AI agent answers and where your data goes. It narrows what the AI can see to what that person may see, and it limits what leaves by sensitivity: Public, Internal or Restricted. It runs on your own hardware.

Q ▸ How is this different from a private cloud or GovCloud AI service?

GovCloud and "private cloud" AI is still someone else's computer — your content and its search index are processed and stored outside your walls, under someone else's controls. Fleet Agentic moves the computer to you instead.

Q ▸ What does it cost?

Two options. On hardware you own: $500/month including 1 seat, then $25 per additional seat per month. Fully leased: $5,000/month flat with as many seats as the hardware supports, including hardware, maintenance, patching, and support. There is no per-token metering on either.

Buyer Questions

Questions your security team will ask

Q ▸ Who patches and audits Fleet Agentic?

Fleet Agentic manages patching, monitoring, and updates of the software under the monthly fee, and of the hardware on the leased tier. Audit logs are available to your team and your assessor.

Q ▸ Does it work in an air-gapped or restricted network?

Yes, on request. Fleet Agentic can be deployed fully disconnected. Managed updates are delivered on a schedule and by a mechanism that fits your environment, including offline transfer for air-gapped enclaves.
